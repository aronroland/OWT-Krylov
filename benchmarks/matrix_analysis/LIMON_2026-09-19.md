# Limon Frozen-Matrix Analysis

## Capture

Original Limon bathymetry, 0-21 m; O1/O1; 36 frequencies, 36 directions;
four MPI ranks; single-precision production solver 13, BiCGSTAB with
rank-local geographic ILU(0). Snapshot: zero-based step 1, dt=600 s,
immediately before the first solve after the one-second initialization/reset.
The physical run completed two steps, with 42 and 19 iterations under SUMWAD
1e-5. Instrumented timings are not performance measurements.

Evidence is retained in
`Triton_C/regtest/steady/limon/solver_runs/matrix-20260919b/`.
The sample is `owt-bicgstab-ilu0-alias13-00`; the snapshot prefix is
`owt-matrix-step-1-stage-0-positive-backbone-pass-0`.
Its `matrix-analysis/analysis.json` records measurements, source hashes and
library versions. `history.json` preserves earlier analysis passes.
The outer runner retains source/binary hashes, commands and resolved settings.
An earlier build in `matrix-20260919` was rejected after concurrent source edits;
it launched no physical simulation and is excluded from these results.

The exported matrix, initial iterate and ILU inverse are checked against live
operator products. Maximum scaled matrix-product discrepancy: 1.974e-7.
Maximum relative L2 inverse discrepancy: 4.706e-8. Offline arithmetic is float64
on the exported float32 coefficients and reciprocal pivots; production precision
and stopping rules are unchanged. Synthetic one-/two-rank tests also check
partition-independent matrix reconstruction and bitwise preservation of the
solver iterate with export enabled.

## Conditioning and Inverse Positivity

Sparse direct solves form q=A^-1 1 in each of the 36 frequency blocks.
All have min(q)=1 and pass the positive-vector criterion: the smallest
rounding-guarded component of Aq is 0.999999999943. Together with the measured
Z-matrix signs, this establishes the numerical M-matrix check on the captured
operator, including its actual boundary rows. It is a floating-point check
with a residual guard, rather than an interval-arithmetic proof.

For an inverse-positive matrix, ||A^-1||inf=max(A^-1 1) and
||A^-1||1=max(A^-T 1). The computed solves give residual-based bounds on these
norms. Taking block maxima before forming products gives whole-system values:

| Whole row-normalized system | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| ||A||1 | 3.275961 |
| ||A^-1||1 | 97.344501 |
| kappa_1(A) | 318.896812 |
| ||A||inf | 5.552435 |
| ||A^-1||inf | 997.402554 |
| kappa_inf(A) | 5538.013023 |

The condition bounds' widths are below 4e-9 and 7e-7 respectively, including
the recorded roundoff allowance. The largest infinity-norm sensitivity occurs
in the lowest-frequency block, whose RHS is exactly zero here. For the
residual-dominant blocks 27-35, individual kappa_inf(A_f) ranges from 505.192
down to 39.349. Global conditioning alone does not identify the active modes.

Lanczos estimates of singular extremes use A^T A and A^-T A^-1; direct sparse
solves apply the inverse. For B=A P^-1, B^-1=P A^-1. All recorded relative
eigenpair residuals are below 9.1e-9. These are converged extremal estimates,
not interval-certified singular-value enclosures.

For block 35, the real extent of the numerical range of B is
[-0.044317, 1.944741]. Because B is real and the symmetric part has both signs,
the numerical range contains zero. The unpreconditioned symmetric part instead
has minimum eigenvalue +0.051330. Thus ILU improves singular-value conditioning
while losing positive definiteness of the symmetric part in the Euclidean
inner product. A condition-number improvement is not sufficient to predict
a proportional iteration reduction.

## What ILU Misses

Let P=L U denote the exported rank-local ILU inverse's effective factors and
let B=A P^-1. Then

\[
B-I=(A-P)P^{-1}.
\]

Decompose A-P into cross-rank, spectral, local boundary-trace and remaining
local factor terms. For each term F, evaluate ||F P^-1 r0||2 / ||r0||2:

| Contribution | Relative norm |
| --- | ---: |
| Local factor defect | 1.060494 |
| Cross-rank couplings | 0.382490 |
| Spectral couplings | 0.048564 |
| Local boundary-trace couplings | 0 |
| Combined defect | 1.136012 |

These norms are not additive percentages. The completed factor audit separates
geographic dropped fill from error on the original factor pattern, retaining
structurally stored zero entries. Across blocks 0, 24 and 35, the maximum
absolute retained-pattern error is 8.895e-8 and its relative Frobenius norm
is at most 5.131e-9. Every local geographic entry belongs to the stored pattern.
For block 35, LU contains 70,268 nonzero fill entries outside that pattern.
Their action on P^-1 r0 has relative norm 1.017794; retained-pattern error
contributes 1.682e-8. The large local defect is dropped fill, while the retained
entries match ILU(0) to production arithmetic accuracy on these audited blocks.
The independent inverse replay agrees to 4.706e-8.
In particular, one unscaled preconditioned Richardson correction would increase
this residual norm by 13.6%. A near-identity approximation is not justified.

## Nonnormal Transport

The highest-frequency block, index 35 at 1.12410 Hz, carries 18.3594% of the
squared initial residual. Its largest strongly connected component has two
unknowns, and its condensation graph has a longest dependency path of 173 blocks.
The union graph of A and P also has only 1x1/2x2 strongly connected components.
Consequently both matrices admit a common block-triangular permutation, and the
complete spectrum of B follows from A_cc P_cc^-1 on those diagonal blocks.

For all 64,008 eigenvalues of this block:

| Operator | Complete real eigenvalue interval | Maximum imaginary part |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| A | [0.842600, 1.157400] | 0 |
| A P^-1 | [0.876181, 1.123819] | 0 |

The completed Lanczos estimate gives ||B-I||2 = 1.742215, whereas
rho(B-I)=0.123819. A normal matrix would have equality between these quantities.
The large difference establishes strong nonnormality; eigenvalue clustering
alone therefore gives a misleading impression of preconditioner quality.
Long off-diagonal transport dependencies remain after preconditioning.

The graph supplies an exact algebraic alternative: a permutation into SCC order
makes A block triangular, so exact diagonal-block solves and forward substitution
solve that system exactly. This is an algebraic statement, not an MPI speedup
claim. Even within the residual-dominant band 27-35, other blocks have SCCs up to
1,972 unknowns; lower-frequency blocks reach 46,680. The two-node result must not
be generalized to every frequency or later physical states.

Both mathematical replays use the same complete B, initial residual and factors.
GMRES is unrestarted with two-pass orthogonalization; BiCGSTAB is SciPy's standard
recurrence. Residuals are recomputed explicitly. Counts below exclude diagnostic
residual checks and report A applications, each paired with P^-1.

| First relative residual crossing | GMRES iterations / A applications | BiCGSTAB iterations / A applications |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1e-2 | 17 / 17 | 11 / 22 |
| 1e-3 | 24 / 24 | 13 / 26 |
| 1e-4 | 27 / 27 | 18 / 36 |
| 1e-5 | 29 / 29 | 20 / 40 |

At 30 applications, residuals are 1.7464e-6 for GMRES and 3.0996e-4 for BiCGSTAB.
At 40 applications, they are 2.1936e-10 and 5.9763e-6 respectively.
GMRES uses 27.5% fewer preconditioned applications to first cross 1e-5 on this
snapshot. Its orthogonalization, memory and communication costs are additional.
This establishes an advantage in residual reduction per operator application,
not a production wall-time winner or a change to SUMWAD acceptance.

## Controlled Removal of Dropped Fill

Let G contain exactly the geographic entries retained by the rank-local
preconditioner, including the original full diagonal. A sparse direct
factorization of G removes dropped fill while preserving the existing MPI,
spectral and trace omissions. This changes one algebraic approximation only.

For block 35, at true relative residual 1e-5:

| Method | Original ILU(0) | Exact same local geographic blocks |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| BiCGSTAB iterations / A applications | 18 / 36 | 6 / 12 |
| GMRES iterations / A applications | 28 / 28 | 11 / 11 |
| kappa_2 of right-preconditioned operator | 13.835 | 10.427 |

The threefold BiCGSTAB iteration reduction is much larger than the condition-
number improvement. Conversely, block 24's condition number increases from
36.498 to 55.798 under the exact local solve, while BiCGSTAB reaches 1e-5 in
14 iterations versus more than 20 with the original ILU. The actual RHS and
residual polynomial matter alongside operator-wide conditioning.

The complete-system control retains all 2,304,288 unknowns and one common
Krylov polynomial. The exact geographic solve's relative residual is 7.372e-16.
G has 6,942,640 nonzeros; its direct L/U factors have 13,288,349 stored entries
(including both factor diagonals). The results at true relative residual 1e-5
are:

| Complete-system method | Original ILU(0) | Exact same local geographic blocks | Reduction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| BiCGSTAB iterations | 20 | 12 | 40.0% |
| BiCGSTAB A / inverse applications | 40 | 24 | 40.0% |
| GMRES A / inverse applications | 29 | 22 | 24.1% |

For exact local blocks, first BiCGSTAB crossings of 1e-2, 1e-3, 1e-4 and
1e-5 occur at 4, 7, 9 and 12 iterations. GMRES reaches those thresholds in
7, 13, 17 and 22 applications. The original ILU gives BiCGSTAB crossings at
11, 13, 18 and 20 iterations. Counts exclude explicit diagnostic residual
checks and retain the same fixed right-preconditioned equations in each replay.

The single-correction defect ||(A G^-1-I)r0||/||r0|| increases to 1.240375,
compared with 1.136012 for ILU, even as Krylov convergence improves. Thus this
one-step defect is useful for identifying omitted terms, but it is not a
standalone ranking of Krylov preconditioners. The controlled residual histories
provide the causal evidence that removing dropped fill helps this solve.

These complete-system results are in `fill-control.json`; the logged source,
snapshot hashes and library versions accompany them. They establish algebraic
iteration savings. The exact local solve has a different factorization and
application cost, so 40% fewer iterations is not a measured runtime speedup.

The measured factor defect supports improving the geographic factor
approximation. Dependency-aware ordering and additional fill are implementation
options for this identified error source; their cost and effectiveness require
separate evaluation. A later developed-wave snapshot is required before
extending this startup-state conclusion to the complete Limon run.

Seven repository regressions pass: dense-reference conditioning, singular and
symmetric-part spectra, retained-pattern versus dropped-fill separation,
preservation of control omissions, and the existing operator/partition checks.
Their commands and retained logs are documented in README.md.

Reproduction commands and mathematical definitions are in [README.md](README.md).

## Implemented Level-One Fill

The retained replay `build/matrix-fill/limon-fill1-20260919/results.json`
uses the production `IluLevelPreconditioner` on the same captured geographic
matrix. The right-hand side, geographic ordering, complete outer operator,
and omitted cross-rank/spectral terms remain unchanged. The replay uses
float64 arithmetic; it is separate from production timings and SUMWAD checks.

| True relative residual 1e-5 | ILU(0) | ILU(1) | Exact geographic control |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| BiCGSTAB iterations | 20 | 13 | 12 |
| BiCGSTAB operator applications | 40 | 26 | 24 |
| GMRES operator applications | 29 | 22 | 22 |
| Stored component factor values | 15,018,048 | 25,108,704 | Different sparse representation |

The level-one pattern uses 67.19% more stored component values and removes
seven of the eight excess BiCGSTAB iterations relative to the exact-local
control on this snapshot. This supports the implemented fill change for this
system; it does not establish the result for developed states or Duck.
Recomputed ILU(0)'s inverse action differs from the exported float factors
by 6.15e-9 in relative L2. Source hashes remained unchanged during the replay.

The new symbolic pattern follows
`level(i,j) = min(level(i,j), level(i,p) + level(p,j) + 1)` and retains levels
at most k. Numeric refresh zeroes every fill slot before refactorization.
Independent dense symbolic elimination and dot-product factors verify levels
0, 1 and 2, float/double, three components, ghost exclusion, refresh and aliasing.
Triton exposes levels 0/1 for its existing geographic ILU solver IDs through
`ilu_fill_level`; its default remains zero. The interface coupling remains
outside these local factors, explicitly recorded as rank-local geographic ILU.
